# Adoption of the @typing.override decorator proposal

- Status: accepted
- Decision ID: D-e2e0269b
- Sources: https://discuss.python.org/t/pep-698-a-typing-override-decorator/20839
- Participants: brettcannon, stroxler
- Traceability: 3 of 3 quotes found verbatim in the source

## Decision

The `@typing.override` decorator was accepted with the name as proposed.

## Why

The name was chosen to be consistent with existing typing decorators and to clearly indicate the purpose of the decorator.
