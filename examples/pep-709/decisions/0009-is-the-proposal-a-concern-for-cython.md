# Is the proposal a concern for Cython?

- Status: open
- Decision ID: D-d0843400
- Sources: https://discuss.python.org/t/pep-709-inlined-comprehensions/24240
- Participants: da-woods
- Traceability: 1 of 1 quotes found verbatim in the source

## Decision

The Steering Council has not explicitly decided if the PEP is a concern for Cython, but Cython developers are relaxed about it.

## Why

The Cython developers are not concerned about the changes described in the PEP, as they are mostly focused on Python 3.6 compatibility and the changes do not significantly impact Cython's functionality.

### Evidence

> "I don’t think this PEP is a real concern for Cython. It’s the sort of minor detail where we’re generally relaxed if we don’t quite hit."
>
> da-woods, [post 35](https://discuss.python.org/t/pep-709-inlined-comprehensions/24240/35) (verified)
